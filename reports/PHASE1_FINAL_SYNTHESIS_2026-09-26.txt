2. Walk-forward
No strategy was Pareto-dominant across both common and expansive walk-forward histories. B0/B1 had strong common-history medians but severe expansive-history drawdowns. Tactical families also showed regime variation. S6 was especially unstable across folds.

3. Parameter-neighbor sensitivity
Neighbor tests did not identify a parameter setting for selection. Results were used only as stability diagnostics.

4. Universe sensitivity
Leave-one-asset-out results showed meaningful dependence on the investable universe. S6 was exceptionally sensitive; other tactical families also moved materially when assets were removed. This prevents treating the original universe result as universal.

5. Costs/slippage
The pre-registered six friction scenarios did not change the broad conclusions. S1/S3 were moderately more friction-sensitive than B0/B1; S5 also showed moderate sensitivity; S6's CAGR changed little under the tested friction assumptions but its drawdown/universe dependence remained.

6. Concentration/dependence
Dependence diagnostics confirmed that some strategy outcomes are driven materially by individual assets. The diagnostic was not used to reweight or select a strategy.

9. Statistical uncertainty
Circular moving-block bootstrap of monthly time-weighted returns; 3/6/12-month blocks; 6-month primary; 5,000 replicates; 95% percentile intervals. Common history contained 71 months; expansive history 319 months.
Primary paired CAGR differences versus B0 had 95% intervals crossing zero for every comparator in both histories. Block-length sensitivity changed interval width but not the broad conclusion.
Therefore the observed historical differences are not sufficiently separated to establish a universal strategy-family winner under this uncertainty model.

FINAL CONCLUSION
No robust Phase 1 winner has been established.

The evidence supports the following factual conclusions:
- Buy & Hold and DCA are strong benchmarks in the original universe, but their expansive-history drawdowns are very large and their results do not generalize consistently to the additional universes.
- Momentum and Rotation are relatively consistent across several development tests and show materially different behavior from the benchmarks in unseen universes, but they do not dominate across all frozen dimensions.
- Dynamic Allocation shows comparatively constrained drawdowns in several tested universes and histories, but its returns are lower than the strongest benchmark/tactical observations and it is not dominant across all tests.
- Moving Average is inconsistent across histories and regimes.
- Risk Parity is highly dependent on universe composition and can experience very large drawdowns.
- Statistical uncertainty is broad enough that pairwise CAGR differences do not provide clean separation.
- Value remains an omitted family because the required comparable point-in-time valuation data are unavailable; it is not judged as a failure.
