INTERPRETATION
C2 is the only ensemble with materially more controlled drawdown than C1/C3 and reasonable behavior across both histories. However, it does not consistently improve on its strongest constituent, S5 Dynamic Allocation: S5 had lower development drawdowns and lower expansive walk-forward median drawdown. C2 also does not provide a consistent return/Sharpe improvement across both histories.

Therefore no ensemble meets the project's robustness standard for promotion as a Phase 1 benchmark.

LIMITATION
Because no ensemble survived the common/expansive development plus walk-forward robustness gate, later Phase 1B tests (cost sensitivity, external universe, reserved holdout, bootstrap) were not used to promote a candidate. This is a screening conclusion, not evidence that later tests would have produced identical results.

DECISION
Do not add ensemble complexity to Phase 2 as a research-derived default.
Proceed to Phase 2 separately, carrying Phase 1 families as descriptive reference benchmarks only.
