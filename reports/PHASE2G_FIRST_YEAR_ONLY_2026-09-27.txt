Design
- USD 1,000 on the first observed trading day of each of the first 12 calendar months.
- USD 12,000 total contributions, then zero new contributions for approximately 9 additional years.
- All frozen S1-S4 definitions reused without modification.
- No rebalancing overlay.
- 5% cash floor, D3 deepest-first allocation, costs and execution rules unchanged.

Interpretation
The result confirms that recurring-contribution CAGR cannot be extrapolated to a "contribute for one year, then hold" scenario. The historical post-contribution growth rates are highly dispersed and the expanding-history cohorts contain severe drawdowns. S1 and S3 are nearly identical in this test; S4 is close; S2 has materially lower historical growth but also lower median drawdown. These are descriptive historical observations, not expected future returns and do not select a Phase 2 winner.
