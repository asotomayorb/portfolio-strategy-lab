## Phase 1 versus Phase 2
Phase 1 did not establish a robust universal winner. Main common-history profiles were approximately:
- B0 Buy & Hold: 31.3% CAGR / -36.0% DD.
- B1 DCA: 29.6% / -31.5%.
- S5 Dynamic Allocation: 22.3% / -21.2%.

Phase 2 S1-S4 produce about 27.9%-28.5% CAGR with about -25.5% to -29.2% drawdown on their common recurring-contribution history. These are descriptive comparisons, not a direct winner test because mechanics, universes and evaluation structures differ.

## Decision-oriented interpretation
Using the stated priorities of CAGR, then drawdown, then implementation simplicity, the corrected evidence produces two descriptive pairs:

1. S1/S3: ~27.9% CAGR and ~-29.2% DD.
2. S2/S4: ~28.4%-28.5% CAGR and ~-25.5% to -25.7% DD.

The robustness gate does not establish the second profile as a statistically robust improvement: bootstrap probabilities remain near 50%, confidence intervals are wide, and external/holdout differences are small.

For implementation, S2 and S4 differ in how capital is split while ATR8 is active; S1 and S3 retain the dip component. Phase 2F provides no evidence that taxable sales for rebalancing improve the recurring-contribution baseline.
