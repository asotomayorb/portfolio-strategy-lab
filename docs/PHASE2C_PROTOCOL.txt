PHASE 2C — INITIAL / EXTRAORDINARY CAPITAL DEPLOYMENT SPEEDS
===============================================================

Status: FROZEN before execution.

Purpose
-------
Test how a pre-existing initial capital balance should be phased into the frozen Phase 2 entry engine. This stage does not change the ATR/HH52 trigger definitions, S1-S4 strategy definitions, D3 simultaneous-capital rule, costs, or cash-floor definition.

Pre-registered deployment variants
----------------------------------
- immediate: full initial capital available from day 1.
- 3m: equal scheduled release over 3 months.
- 6m: equal scheduled release over 6 months.
- 12m: equal scheduled release over 12 months.
- 24m: equal scheduled release over 24 months.
- opportunities-only: initial capital remains cash until eligible strategy purchases occur; no time-based forced deployment.

Initial-capital test amount
---------------------------
USD 12,000, equal to 12 months of the frozen USD 1,000 recurring contribution. This normalized amount is used so deployment-speed results can be compared without relying on an unspecified user-specific transfer amount.

Frozen common conditions
------------------------
- Initial capital = USD 12,000.
- Recurring contribution = USD 1,000/month.
- No leverage or shorting.
- 5% cash floor.
- D3 deepest-first simultaneous allocation.
- Orders execute at next valid session open.
- Phase 1 baseline costs: 10 bps commission + 5 bps slippage.
- Same universe and target allocations as Stage 2A.
- Same common and expanding histories.
- All S1-S4 are tested.
- Initial capital is introduced only according to the deployment schedule; recurring contributions retain Stage 2A behavior.
- Unused cash carries indefinitely.
- Opportunities-only means no calendar-based release and therefore no forced deployment simply because time elapsed.

Interpretation
--------------
Deployment speed is evaluated descriptively across return, drawdown, risk-adjusted metrics, cash utilization, trade count and the consistency of behavior across common/expanding histories.

No variant is promoted from one metric or one history. Results must subsequently pass Phase 2E robustness checks.

Anti-optimization
-----------------
These six deployment schedules are frozen before execution. No schedule may be added, removed or altered after seeing results in order to improve a preferred candidate.

Relationship to Stage 2A
------------------------
Stage 2A remains the clean contribution-only entry-engine test. Stage 2C does not replace or overwrite it. The 2C results answer a separate question: how the same frozen engine behaves when a pre-existing capital balance must be deployed.
