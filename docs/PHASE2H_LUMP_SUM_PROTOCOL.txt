PHASE 2H — LUMP SUM / TEN-YEAR HOLD
Status: post-Phase-2 descriptive extension.
Date: 2026-09-27

PURPOSE
Measure the historical growth of a single initial USD 12,000 investment with zero
future contributions over an approximately 10-year cohort. This is a separate
sensitivity and must not be inferred from recurring-contribution CAGR or Phase 2G.

SCOPE
- Test all frozen S1-S4 candidates.
- No frozen Phase 2 trigger, allocation, D3, cash-floor, cost or execution rule is changed.
- No rebalancing overlay.
- This extension is descriptive and does not select a Phase 2 winner.
- Historical results are not forecasts or expected future returns.

LUMP-SUM ADAPTATION
- USD 12,000 is deposited on the first observed trading day of each cohort.
- Zero future contributions.
- To preserve the frozen 50% DCA / 50% opportunity structure in a meaningful
  single-deposit setting, the DCA half (USD 6,000) is deployed immediately,
  target-weighted, at the first valid session open.
- The remaining USD 6,000 is retained as the opportunity sleeve and follows the
  frozen Dip/ATR8 allocation logic of each strategy.
- S1: immediate 50% target-weighted sleeve + 50% Dip.
- S2: immediate 50% target-weighted sleeve + 50% ATR8.
- S3: immediate 50% target-weighted sleeve + 25% Dip + 25% ATR8 when ATR8 is active; when no ATR8 is active, use the S1 fallback adaptation.
- S4: immediate 50% target-weighted sleeve + remaining 50% ATR8 when active,
  otherwise the S1 opportunity behavior.
- This is a registered lump-sum adaptation, not a post-result optimization.

HORIZON
- Approximately 10 calendar years per cohort.
- Annual historical cohort starts plus the most recent available decade.
- Full common-history 10-year cohorts are unavailable at the current 2026 cutoff;
  expanding-history repository data are therefore used.

TRADING ENGINE
- Daily trigger evaluation.
- Weekly HH52 from confirmed weekly swing highs using 3 candles each side.
- Wilder/RMA ATR20W.
- ATR% = ATR20W / HH52.
- Trigger multiples 1.5 / 3 / 5 / 8.
- Cumulative targets 25% / 50% / 75% / 100%.
- One cumulative order when multiple levels are crossed.
- Normal room = max(0, target - current - pending).
- D3 deepest-first simultaneous-capital allocation.
- 5% cash floor.
- Next valid session open execution.
- Unused cash carries.
- Costs: 10 bps commission + 5 bps slippage.
- No leverage or short positions.

METRICS
- Total-period CAGR.
- Final equity.
- Final-equity multiple versus USD 12,000.
- Maximum drawdown.
- Sharpe.
- Cash utilization.
- Cohort median and range.

INTERPRETATION
- Do not call historical CAGR an expected return.
- Compare cohort dispersion rather than extrapolating one cohort.
- Phase 2H is not a replacement for frozen Phase 2.
- Phase 2H is directly comparable to the “capital available now, then wait” question,
  but not to a recurring-contribution CAGR.
