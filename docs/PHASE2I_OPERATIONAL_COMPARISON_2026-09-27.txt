PHASE 2I — OPERATIONAL COMPARISON: S2 VS S4
================================================
Date: 2026-09-27
Status: decision-support extension after completed Phase 2 robustness gate.

PURPOSE
-------
Translate the completed Phase 2, 2F, 2G and 2H evidence into an operational
comparison of the two materially distinct Phase 2 profiles retained for human
decision: S2 and S4.

This document does not declare a statistical or historical winner. It freezes
the factual differences that matter for implementation.

CANDIDATES
----------
S2 = 50% DCA + 50% ATR8 when ATR8 is active; otherwise S1 (50% DCA + 50% Dip).
S4 = 100% ATR8 when ATR8 is active; otherwise S1
     (50% DCA + 50% Dip).

COMMON FROZEN MECHANICS
-----------------------
- Current portfolio targets from portfolio_allocation.csv.
- No leverage and no shorting.
- Daily trigger evaluation.
- Weekly HH52 using confirmed weekly swing highs with 3 weekly candles on each side.
- Wilder/RMA 20-week ATR.
- ATR trigger levels: 1.5 / 3 / 5 / 8.
- Cumulative target levels: 25% / 50% / 75% / 100%.
- One cumulative order when several levels are crossed in one day.
- Normal room = max(0, target - current position - pending orders).
- D3 deepest-first simultaneous-capital allocation.
- 5% cash floor.
- Next-session-open execution.
- Unused cash carries.
- 10 bps commission + 5 bps slippage.
- Contribution-only reweighting: future DCA contributions correct current
  underweights first; existing holdings are not sold.

OPERATIONAL DIFFERENCE
----------------------
S2:
1. Half of the monthly contribution is the DCA sleeve.
2. The other half is reserved for ATR8 opportunities.
3. When an ATR8 trigger occurs, eligible capital is allocated under D3. When no ATR8 is active, the opportunity sleeve follows S1's four-level Dip behavior.
4. When ATR8 is active, the strategy does not switch to the broader four-level Dip mechanism; when no ATR8 is active, it behaves exactly as S1.

S4:
1. When at least one ATR8 opportunity is active, the full strategy allocation
   is directed to ATR8 opportunities under D3.
2. When no ATR8 opportunity is active, S4 falls back to S1:
   50% DCA + 50% Dip across the four cumulative ATR levels.
3. Therefore S4 has a conditional state transition that S2 does not have.

MONTHLY CONTRIBUTION / NO-SALE RULE
-----------------------------------
For the recurring-contribution implementation:
- USD 1,000 arrives monthly.
- The DCA sleeve is directed first toward assets below target weight.
- Any unused DCA budget is allocated by target weight.
- No existing position is sold solely to rebalance.
- ATR8/dip opportunity capital remains subject to the frozen allocation-room
  and simultaneous-trigger rules.
- Cash not deployed remains available for future opportunities.

This is the validated Phase 2F contribution-only baseline.

COMMON-HISTORY EVIDENCE
-----------------------
Contribution-only baseline, 2020-09-30 to 2026-09-24:

S2:
- CAGR: 28.01%
- Maximum drawdown: -25.20%
- Sharpe: 1.110

S4:
- CAGR: 28.13%
- Maximum drawdown: -27.85%
- Sharpe: 1.091

The observed CAGR difference is 0.12 percentage points in favor of S4.
The observed maximum-drawdown difference is 2.65 percentage points, with S2
having the smaller drawdown.

Phase 2E bootstrap uncertainty did not establish robust separation among S1-S4.
Therefore these common-history differences are descriptive and must not be
interpreted as a proven superiority of either candidate.

REBAlANCING / SALES EVIDENCE
----------------------------
Phase 2F compared the contribution-only baseline with hybrid20 and hybrid30,
which add sales when holdings exceed target weight by the registered threshold.

In the common history:
- Adding sales materially reduced CAGR.
- Maximum drawdown changed very little.
- Sharpe increased modestly.
- Turnover became substantial.

Therefore the evidence does not support replacing contribution-only reweighting
with recurring sales for this use case. Hybrid sales remain an optional
implementation sensitivity rather than a research-selected improvement.

FIRST-YEAR-ONLY AND LUMP-SUM CAPITAL
------------------------------------
Phase 2G and 2H show that the financing schedule materially changes the
historical outcomes.

Phase 2G:
- USD 1,000/month for 12 months, then zero contributions.
- S2 median post-contribution CAGR: 42.42%.
- S4 median post-contribution CAGR: 59.40%.
- S2 median final-equity multiple: 25.21x.
- S4 median final-equity multiple: 88.30x.
- These are 18 historical cohorts and descriptive, not forecasts.

Phase 2H:
- USD 12,000 initially, then zero contributions.
- S2 median CAGR: 51.09%.
- S4 median CAGR: 67.50%.
- S2 median final-equity multiple: 62.88x.
- S4 median final-equity multiple: 175.39x.
- These are 18 historical cohorts and descriptive, not forecasts.

These scenarios must not be merged with the recurring-contribution CAGR. They
answer different capital-deployment questions and have high cohort dependence.

IMPLEMENTATION TRADE-OFF
------------------------
S2 has the simpler state machine:
- fixed 50/50 DCA + ATR8 structure;
- no conditional fallback.

S4 has a more conditional state machine:
- ATR8-active state -> 100% ATR8;
- no ATR8-active state -> S1 behavior.

Thus implementation simplicity is a factual difference even though both use the
same underlying trigger, target-room and D3 mechanisms.

DECISION FRAME
--------------
The completed research leaves two non-equivalent profiles:

S2:
- lower observed common-history maximum drawdown;
- slightly lower observed common-history CAGR;
- simpler rule structure.

S4:
- slightly higher observed common-history CAGR;
- somewhat higher observed common-history maximum drawdown;
- more conditional implementation.

No metric is designated as a winner. The human decision is to choose which
operating profile to freeze, if any, while accepting that Phase 2E did not
provide robust statistical separation.

PHASE 3 GATE
------------
Phase 3 remains blocked until the human freezes:
1. S2 or S4 (or explicitly decides not to proceed);
2. contribution-only reweighting versus an explicitly chosen sales overlay;
3. recurring-contribution operating mode and, if needed, separate treatment of
   initial/lump-sum capital;
4. the final cash-floor and execution assumptions already frozen by research.

No Phase 2 definitions are reopened by this document.
