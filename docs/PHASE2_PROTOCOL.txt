PHASE 2 — USER-SPECIFIC ATR/PULLBACK STRATEGY
===============================================

STATUS
ACTIVE — Stage 2A entry-engine research.
Phase 1 and Phase 1B are closed and must not be modified.

PURPOSE
Evaluate the user's specified ATR/pullback investment mechanics separately from generic Phase 1 strategy families.

FROZEN CORE
Universe/targets: current portfolio_allocation.csv.
No leverage; no short.
Daily trigger evaluation.
Weekly structural inputs:
- HH52 based on confirmed weekly swing highs.
- Swing high requires 3 weekly candles on each side.
- A swing high becomes usable only after the 3 right-side weeks have completed.
- HH52 is the maximum usable confirmed swing high in the trailing 52 weeks.
- ATR is Wilder/RMA 20-week ATR.
- ATR and HH52 are weekly; price trigger is checked daily.
- ATR% = ATR20W / HH52.
- Trigger prices = HH52 * (1 - k * ATR%), k = 1.5, 3, 5, 8.
- Cumulative target position at deepest triggered level:
  1.5x = 25% of target
  3x = 50%
  5x = 75%
  8x = 100%.
- Each daily evaluation uses only the deepest triggered level. Crossing multiple levels in one day creates one cumulative order, not multiple orders.
- Normal order room = MAX(0, target position - current position - pending orders).
- S1 ATR8 is capped at the asset target; no indefinite over-allocation in the frozen S1 baseline.
- Orders are executed at the next session open.
- Unused cash carries indefinitely; deposits do not expire and do not force a purchase.
- Costs use Phase 1 baseline: 10 bps commission + 5 bps slippage.

S1–S4 STRATEGY MATRIX
S1 = 50% DCA + 50% Dip.
  Dip uses all four cumulative ATR levels.
S2 = 50% DCA + 50% ATR8 when ATR8 is active; otherwise S1 (50% DCA + 50% Dip).
S3 = 50% DCA + 25% Dip + 25% ATR8.
S4 = 100% ATR8 when ATR8 is active; otherwise S1.

DCA means the specified DCA capital is deployed according to target allocations, subject to allocation room.
Dip/ATR8 orders are pooled globally when multiple assets trigger on the same day.

UNRESOLVED SIMULTANEOUS-TRIGGER DIMENSION
The exact global distribution rule was not previously frozen. Therefore Stage 2A tests three pre-registered descriptive variants:
D1 target-weighted allocation among eligible needs.
D2 equal-weight allocation among eligible needs.
D3 deepest-first allocation, then residual by target weight.
No result may be used to choose or tune the rule until all three are reported.

STAGE 2A ISOLATION
To avoid contaminating entry mechanics with the still-unresolved initial-capital deployment policy:
- Initial capital = 0 for the entry-engine comparison.
- Monthly contribution = 1,000.
- Contributions are available as cash; no forced purchase outside the strategy's DCA/dip rules.
- Baseline cash floor = 5%.
- This stage measures the incremental contribution process. Initial-capital deployment is a separate later stage.

PRIMARY OUTPUT
Compare S1–S4 across D1–D3 on the same data, with common and expansive histories.
Report CAGR/TWR-style return, maximum drawdown, Sharpe, Sortino, cash utilization, trigger counts, and execution/trade counts.
Do not declare a winner from one metric.
