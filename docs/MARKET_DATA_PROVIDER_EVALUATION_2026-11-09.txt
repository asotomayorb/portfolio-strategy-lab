MARKET DATA PROVIDER EVALUATION — 2026-11-09
================================================

STATUS
------
Technical evaluation only. No live provider has been connected, no API key is stored in the repository, and no provider is yet approved for production use. This work does not freeze or select a Phase 2 strategy and does not unblock Phase 3 execution.

CURRENT PORTFOLIO DATA SCOPE
----------------------------
The current portfolio_allocation.csv contains 13 instruments:
- US-listed securities/ETFs: VT, SMH, INDA, MSFT, NVDA, AVGO, GLD, URA, XLE, VHT, PLTR, QQQ.
- Crypto: BTCUSD (normalized to BTC in the historical ticker loader; provider symbol expected to be btcusd).

Cash is not a market-data instrument.

ARCHITECTURE DECISION
---------------------
Market data must come from an external market-data provider, independent of the broker adapter and independent of TradingView.

Keep three separate responsibilities:
1. Market Data Provider: historical OHLC, current reference prices, timestamps, market/session state and data-quality metadata.
2. Broker Adapter (initially IBKR): account positions, cash/buying power, pending orders, contract identifiers, executable quotes/order validation, order submission and fills.
3. Strategy/Order Engine: consumes normalized market data and broker account state; it must not depend on a specific vendor or broker implementation.

External market-data prices are inputs for signals and valuation. They are NOT assumed to be executable prices. Immediately before an order proposal/submission, the broker adapter must independently validate the instrument, trading session, executable bid/ask or relevant order reference, available funds, position and pending orders. Any stale, missing, crossed, malformed or materially discrepant price must block the order and raise a visible data-quality state.

PROVIDER CANDIDATE: TIINGO
--------------------------
Tiingo is the first candidate for a controlled proof of concept, not a final production selection.

Relevant documented capabilities:
- 24x5 Consolidated Real-time US equity REST and WebSocket endpoints, described by Tiingo as available on every self-serve plan, including Starter.
- The consolidated equity stream publishes a derived reference price (tngoLast), not a promise of executable NBBO or a raw full-market trade tape. It is intended for reference pricing, valuation and risk metrics.
- Tiingo Crypto provides consolidated crypto prices, top-of-book and historical intraday OHLC data; BTCUSD is documented as the btcusd pair.
- Individual Starter is listed at USD 0/month and Power at USD 30/month. Starter has lower API limits and five years of price history; Power has higher limits and 15+ years.
- Individual plans are for personal/internal use only. Do not redistribute or expose raw provider data publicly.

Official documentation:
- https://www.tiingo.com/documentation/equity-realtime-stock-data
- https://www.tiingo.com/documentation/websockets/equity-realtime-stock-data
- https://www.tiingo.com/documentation/crypto
- https://www.tiingo.com/pricing
- https://www.tiingo.com/documentation/general/changelog

WHY IT IS THE FIRST CANDIDATE
-----------------------------
It appears to cover the portfolio's two market-data needs with one vendor family: US-listed equity/ETF reference prices and BTCUSD crypto prices. Its current self-serve pricing and limits make a personal-use proof of concept inexpensive. Production data-provider eligibility is based on current reference prices, not on an instrument having 52 weeks of listing/history. Historical data is optional in production; the legacy historical files remain the source for experimental backtests. Any available history may be displayed, including a short history for a newly listed instrument.

UNRESOLVED / MUST PASS BEFORE ADOPTION
--------------------------------------
1. Authenticate and request each of the 12 US-listed symbols individually; record exact symbol, venue/asset type, returned fields and entitlement status. Current reference-price coverage is the gate; do not reject a newly listed instrument because it has less than 52 weeks of history.
2. Authenticate BTCUSD Crypto Prices REST and WebSocket requests; confirm pair metadata, quote currency USD, priceData structure and timestamp semantics. Do not use the deprecated cross-exchange Crypto Top-of-Book REST endpoint for execution bid/ask.
3. Verify the 24x5 US equity REST and WebSocket endpoints return usable reference prices for every holding, including ETFs. For equities, use tngoLast and permit lqRefPrice as a documented equivalent reference-price fallback when tngoLast is null.
4. Measure actual update latency, missing/stale periods, reconnect behavior, rate limits and market-closed behavior over a representative observation period. This is not covered by the current REST-only probe.
5. Historical OHLC is optional for production price display. When history is available, report its actual start/end dates and observation count; do not impose a 52-week minimum on symbol coverage. HH52 must remain unavailable until 52 weeks of valid observations exist; ATR20W must remain unavailable until 20 valid weekly observations exist. Any combined ATR%-to-HH52 signal remains unavailable until both inputs are valid. Do not silently mix adjusted and unadjusted series.
6. Compare selected provider prices with IBKR quotes during overlapping market hours; define instrument-specific discrepancy tolerances before enabling order proposals.
7. Verify the current Tiingo terms and exact plan entitlements before deployment. No public display, redistribution or multi-user use is assumed.
8. Keep provider tokens outside GitHub, source files, logs and browser code. Use environment secrets and redact credentials from errors.

ALTERNATIVES RETAINED
---------------------
- Twelve Data: strongest alternative if broad asset-class coverage is a priority (US/global equities and ETFs, forex, crypto, commodities, indices and fixed income). Basic is free but only 8 API credits/minute and 8 trial WebSocket credits; Grow is listed at USD 79/month and includes 20+ markets, real-time US stocks, EOD global equities/ETFs and commodities. Its US real-time equity feed uses venues representing approximately 5% of US trading volume, so it is not equivalent to full consolidated US tape. It may be the better single-vendor breadth candidate, but not necessarily the better US reference-price feed.
- Massive: broad US-market coverage across equities, indices, forex/crypto and futures. Stocks Basic is free but EOD only; Stocks Starter is USD 29/month and 15-minute delayed; real-time stocks require the higher Stocks Advanced tier (listed at USD 199/month). More expensive for the current low-frequency portfolio-price requirement, but broader for US derivatives/market coverage.
- Alpaca: viable for US equities/ETFs and crypto, but Basic stock feed is IEX-only; all-US-exchange SIP coverage is USD 99/month (Algo Trader Plus). Narrower asset-class breadth than Twelve Data/Massive.

READ-ONLY REST COVERAGE PROBE
-----------------------------
A credential-safe diagnostic has been added at scripts/validate_tiingo_coverage.py. By default it reads portfolio_allocation.csv and checks only current reference-price availability for the 12 US-listed symbols and BTCUSD (13 instruments, one REST request per instrument). It does not query historical data unless explicitly invoked with --include-history. It prints a sanitized JSON summary and never prints or stores the token. Run it locally with TIINGO_API_KEY set in the environment:

  TIINGO_API_KEY=... python scripts/validate_tiingo_coverage.py

Optional history diagnostics:

  TIINGO_API_KEY=... python scripts/validate_tiingo_coverage.py --include-history --start-date 2024-01-01

The probe does not require 250 daily rows or any minimum history length. It does not test WebSocket continuity/latency, does not validate executable prices, and does not authorize order generation. Its pure helper functions have unit tests in tests/test_tiingo_coverage.py. Live REST validation remains pending until a user-owned API key is configured in the execution environment.

PROVIDER FIT CONCLUSION — 2026-11-10
------------------------------------
Decision: CONDITIONAL FIT for the current portfolio, NOT approved as a universal provider.

Tiingo is a reasonable low-cost first provider for the present universe (12 US-listed equities/ETFs plus BTCUSD) if the only required external function is a current reference price for valuation, monitoring and signal inputs. The 24x5 consolidated US equity REST/WebSocket feed is included on Starter and Power; the published Starter allowance (500 unique symbols/month, 50 requests/hour, 1,000/day, 1 GB/month) is ample in principle for a 13-instrument personal portfolio with cached snapshots and a single WebSocket stream. The documented 24x5 reference price is derived from multiple US equity venues, but is not a broker-executable NBBO guarantee.

Tiingo is NOT yet proven to serve every symbol in the portfolio: no authenticated API call has been made. In particular, exact support for each ETF, ticker aliases, newly listed symbols, BTCUSD response structure and actual timestamps remains unverified. The REST probe is only the first coverage gate. The 24x5 feed covers US equities; global securities coverage shown in the product catalog must not be confused with a promise of real-time global exchange quotes. Tiingo should therefore not be described as covering "almost any asset" in real time without additional asset-class/venue evidence.

For the user's stated "any or almost any asset" preference, Twelve Data has broader published asset-class breadth (global equities/ETFs, forex, crypto, commodities, indices and fixed income), but Grow is USD 79/month and its US real-time stock feed represents about 5% of US volume. Massive is a stronger broad US data alternative for indices/futures/currencies, but real-time stocks are materially more expensive. A multi-provider adapter is likely preferable to forcing one vendor to cover every possible asset class.

Recommended architecture:
- Tiingo adapter as a candidate US-equity/ETF reference-price source.
- Tiingo Crypto Prices as BTCUSD last/reference-price only, subject to live validation; never rely on its deprecated cross-exchange Crypto Top-of-Book.
- IBKR adapter remains the sole source of executable quotes, tradability and order validation.
- Add provider routing by asset class/venue later if the asset universe expands beyond US-listed securities and BTC.
- If an instrument has a current reference price but insufficient history, display the price and available history only; do not generate HH52/ATR signals until the relevant windows are valid.

Plan economics: Starter appears sufficient for the current 13 symbols under ordinary polling/caching and one streaming connection. Power at USD 30/month is not justified until a measured need for its larger request/bandwidth quotas or longer history is established. Reconfirm terms and exact endpoint entitlements in the account before production use. Both individual tiers are internal-use-only; public display, redistribution or multi-user use requires separate licensing.

HARD GATES BEFORE APPROVAL
--------------------------
A. User-owned TIINGO_API_KEY configured as an Actions secret or local environment variable; do not send the token in chat or commit it.
B. Authenticated REST run returns a usable reference price and timestamp for every current instrument; capture sanitized result only.
C. Authenticated WebSocket test runs through US regular/extended/overnight sessions, reconnects after forced disconnect, and records update latency, stale gaps and message parsing.
D. BTCUSD Crypto Prices passes REST and WebSocket validation; no reliance on deprecated aggregate top-of-book.
E. Compare overlapping reference prices against IBKR market data and establish per-asset discrepancy/staleness thresholds.
F. Review personal-use licensing and confirm the application is private/internal only.

Until A-F pass, status remains CONDITIONAL / NOT APPROVED.
No live order path or strategy engine integration is authorized by this evaluation.
