RESERVED HOLDOUT DESIGN
Frozen ticker universe, selected before execution and not present as ticker data in prior Phase 1 runs:
VTI, MDY, EFA, IAU, AGG, IEF, TIP, EMB, XLB, XLY.
Frozen window: 2007-01-01 through 2026-09-24.
Allocation: 95% invested, equal weight; 5% cash.
Source: Yahoo Finance daily chart OHLCV at CI runtime.
EMB begins 2007-12-19; all other holdout tickers begin 2007-01-03. The engine's expanding-history treatment handles this coverage difference; no synthetic prehistory was created.
The strategy definitions, parameters, monthly cadence, next-open execution, leverage/short constraints and friction assumptions remained unchanged.
This is a genuinely reserved ticker-universe holdout/generalization test, but not a claim of independent temporal OOS.

INTERPRETATION
The reserved universe again shows that strategy-family behavior depends on the investable universe. Several tactical families have positive and comparatively moderate drawdowns here, but the holdout is one validation sample and does not establish a universal winner. It is descriptive evidence only. Holdout results must not be used for parameter or strategy selection.

STATISTICAL UNCERTAINTY
Method was frozen before execution: circular moving-block bootstrap on monthly time-weighted returns, using the original development universe only. Block lengths 3, 6 and 12 months were all evaluated; 6 months was the primary analysis. 5,000 bootstrap replicates per strategy/history/block length and 95% percentile intervals were used. Contributions were handled as r_t = equity_t / (equity_{t-1} + contribution_t) - 1. Paired CAGR differences used identical bootstrap resamples across strategies. Results are stored in reports/phase1_statistical_uncertainty_primary.csv and reports/phase1_statistical_pairwise_cagr_primary.csv. Primary paired CAGR intervals versus B0 crossed zero for every comparator in both common and expansive histories. Block-length sensitivity did not alter the broad conclusion.
