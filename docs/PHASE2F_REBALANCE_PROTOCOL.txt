PHASE 2F PROTOCOL — HYBRID REBALANCING SENSITIVITY
=====================================================

Status: POST-PHASE-2 EXTENSION
Frozen Phase 2E definitions are not modified.

Purpose
-------
Test whether the previously discussed "contribution-first, sell-only-beyond-threshold"
rebalancing overlay changes the behavior of the frozen Phase 2 candidates.

Candidates
----------
All four frozen Phase 2E candidates are retained because Phase 2E did not establish
a robust universal separation:
- S1: 50% DCA + 50% Dip
- S2: 50% DCA + 50% ATR8
- S3: 50% DCA + 25% Dip + 25% ATR8
- S4: 100% ATR8 when active, otherwise S1

D3 deepest-first simultaneous-capital allocation is retained.

Comparison modes
----------------
1. baseline / contribution-only
   - The monthly USD 1,000 contribution remains unchanged.
   - The DCA half (USD 500) is allocated to current target deficits first.
   - Any unused portion of that DCA budget is allocated by target weight.
   - No existing holdings are sold by the overlay.

2. hybrid20
   - The monthly USD 1,000 contribution remains unchanged.
   - The DCA half (USD 500) is allocated to current target deficits first.
   - Any unused portion of that DCA budget is allocated by target weight.
   - Existing holdings are sold only when current weight is more than 20%
     above target weight.
   - Sales target the amount needed to return the holding to target weight.
   - Sales execute at the next valid session open.

3. hybrid30
   Same as hybrid20, but the sale threshold is more than 30% above target.

The 50% DCA / remaining dip-or-ATR8 split is preserved. No contribution is
created or destroyed by the overlay.

Frozen mechanics retained
-------------------------
- Daily trigger evaluation.
- Weekly HH52 and Wilder/RMA ATR20W.
- Three weekly candles on each side for swing-high confirmation.
- ATR levels 1.5 / 3 / 5 / 8 with cumulative 25 / 50 / 75 / 100%.
- D3 deepest-first allocation.
- 5% cash floor.
- No leverage and no shorting.
- Unused cash carries.
- Next-session-open execution.
- 10 bps commission + 5 bps slippage.
- Historical inputs from tickers/*.csv and portfolio_allocation.csv.

Evaluation
----------
Both common and expanding histories are tested.

Metrics:
- CAGR / contribution-flow-adjusted TWR-derived annualized growth.
- Maximum drawdown.
- Sharpe.
- Cash utilization.
- Number of sell trades caused by the overlay.
- Rebalancing turnover.

Interpretation rules
--------------------
- This is a sensitivity/implementation extension, not a reopening of Phase 2E.
- Baseline control must be validated before interpreting hybrid differences.
- No threshold is selected from the results as an optimized parameter.
- Results are descriptive unless a consistent effect is demonstrated across
  histories and candidates.
- The baseline is specifically the contribution-only implementation: future
  contributions correct underweights first, but no existing positions are sold.
  Therefore the hybrid cases isolate the incremental effect of adding sales
  beyond the contribution-only reweighting rule.
- A rebalancing overlay cannot retroactively change the frozen Phase 2E conclusion.
